💡 Quick grammar notes

아/어요 — polite everyday present tense

-고 싶다 — want to do something

-(으)면 — if / when

-아/어야 하다 — must / have to

-(으)세요 — polite request or instruction

-기 전에 — before doing something

-은/는 — topic marker

-이/가 — subject marker
